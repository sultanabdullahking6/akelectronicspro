Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

AK Electronics Pro ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and phone number when you sign up.
  • Order details: delivery address, phone number, items ordered, and payment method you choose at checkout.
  • If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your basic profile name and email from that provider.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To contact you about your order via WhatsApp, email, or phone.
  • To let you log back in without re-entering your details every time.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third party.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account and order data is securely stored using Supabase, a third-party database provider, protected by access rules that ensure only you (and our store admin) can view your own orders.

Cookies & Local Storage

We store a small preference (such as your dark/light mode choice and shopping cart contents) directly in your browser. This is not shared with anyone and is only used to improve your experience on this site.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below.
