Return & Warranty Policy

Last updated: July 2026

We want every component and project kit to arrive exactly as expected. Here's how returns and warranty claims work.

Return Window

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery if an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or defective.
  • Message us on WhatsApp or email with your order number and a photo/video of the issue — this speeds up the process significantly.
  • Items must be unused and in their original packaging to qualify for a return.

What's Covered

  • Components that don't power on or function as described, out of the box.
  • Wrong item sent compared to what was ordered.
  • Missing parts from a bundled kit or exhibition project.

What's Not Covered

  • Damage caused by incorrect wiring, wrong voltage, static discharge, or reversed polarity after the item has left our hands.
  • Components that were soldered, modified, or integrated into a custom circuit.
  • Change-of-mind returns once a custom or made-to-order exhibition kit has been assembled.

Refund or Replacement

Once a return is approved, you can choose between a free replacement (subject to stock) or a refund via your original payment method (JazzCash/EasyPaisa) or bank transfer. Cash on Delivery refunds are processed via JazzCash/EasyPaisa or store credit.

Warranty on Modules & Boards

Arduino, ESP32/ESP8266, and Raspberry Pi boards are tested before dispatch and covered against manufacturing defects for 7 days from delivery. Sensors and passive components (resistors, wires, breadboards) are sold as-is once confirmed working at handover, given their sensitivity to handling.

How to Start a Claim

WhatsApp us at +92 304 6939028 or email orders@akelectronics.com with your order number, a short description, and a photo or short video showing the issue.
